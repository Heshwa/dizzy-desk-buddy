# Dizzy V2 wiring and first power-up

Read [the connection table](wiring.csv) by signal. Treat the listed pins, fuse, connectors and mechanical dimensions as first prototype interfaces. Confirm component markings, polarity and the final checkout total before buying.

- Fit the protected 2S pack to the fused master supply. Keep the removable charge lead separate and use the pack maker's approved charger. Verify charger plug and polarity. Use the motor cut-off to disconnect the motor converter input independently of firmware. Feed motor and logic branches separately from the common fused pack and return them to the same ground point.
- With the motion firmware disabled by default, verify each 5 V regulator at maximum and minimum usable pack voltage, then at measured electronics and servo loads. Scope startup, sudden stop, blocked actuator and lost-I²C cases. A 6 A peak demand is already possible in the model. Use a current-limited bench supply for initial rig measurements. Confirm the pack and switch ratings against the measured profile before battery operation.
- Verify the 3.3 V I²C bus with one effective pull-up pair and the body expansion disconnected. The board's internal bus includes audio at 0x40. The body PCA9685 uses 0x40 on GPIO17/18. Set the body BMI270 to 0x69. Ensure the RTC backup circuit matches the supplied cell, and confirm alarms survive pack removal.
- Check the head UART uses 3.3 V levels. Enable the USB Serial/JTAG console when building the S3 firmware so GPIO43/44 are reserved for the audio bridge. Set the amplifier's intended channel selection and verify the isolated chest speaker. Verify the polarity and coil ratings for both speakers.
- Wire the cut-off contact so a mechanical push removes servo power without software. Verify OE remains high through reset and firmware failure. Mount the torso IMU level with its positive Z axis up. Fit both foot contacts so they reliably register on a level floor. The head IMU moves with the neck.
- Verify neutral pulse lengths and accessible physical stops with the servos unloaded. Update the default trim for the actual horns in the firmware configuration before fitting the links. Then test axes individually in a support jig, followed by a four-leg-servo gait rig carrying the proposed upper assembly mass.
- Use the motor and floor-enable options only after their respective bench checks. For untethered testing, use a level floor and the battery pack. A raised desk is for the seated dock.
